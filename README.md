
# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

This project analyzes agricultural production, resource use, environmental conditions, and economic performance across crops, seasons, locations, and irrigation methods.

## Problem Statement

Agricultural activities are influenced by seasonal variations in environmental conditions, farming practices, resource availability and market conditions. As a result, agricultural performance may differ from one season to another. However, raw agricultural data does not clearly explain how agricultural performance changes across seasons or what patterns can be observed in different seasonal conditions. The problem is to analyze the given agricultural dataset and investigate seasonal differences in agricultural performance by identifying meaningful patterns, trends, relationships and variations within the available data.

### Importance of the Problem Statement

Seasonal variations can influence different aspects of agricultural activities and performance. Understanding seasonal patterns through data analytics can help stakeholders:

1.  Understand variations in agricultural performance
2.  Identify important seasonal trends
3.  Compare performance across different periods
4.  Understand changing environmental conditions
5.  Examine differences in resource usage
6.  Identify areas requiring further investigation
7.  Support evidence-based agricultural planning

## Objectives

- Clean and understand the agricultural dataset.
- Compare performance across seasons and crops.
- Analyse water, fertilizer, pesticide, and irrigation use.
- Examine environmental relationships with yield.
- Evaluate revenue, cost, and profit.
- Use correlation and statistical testing to identify meaningful differences.
- Produce evidence-based findings and recommendations.

## Key Findings

The following findings are generated from the results of the analysis.

1.  The season with the highest average profit is Kharif with an average profit of ₹ 178914.65
2.  The most profitable crop is Sugarcane with an average profit of ₹ 817187.99
3.  The crop with the highest average yield is Sugarcane with 46.94 tonnes per hectare.
4.  The most water-efficient irrigation method is Rainfed with an average water efficiency of 7.56 tonnes per 1,000 m³.
5.  The environmental variable most strongly related to yield is Rainfall_mm with correlation value 0.031
6.  The season with the lowest average profit is Zaid

## Recommendations

1.  Farmers should study the practices used during Kharif because it had the highest average profit.
2.  The use of Rainfed irrigation can be encouraged where suitable because it showed the best water efficiency.
3.  Farmers growing Wheat should focus on reducing input costs and improving market planning.
4.  Agricultural planning should closely monitor Rainfall_mm because it showed the strongest relationship with crop yield.
5.  More attention should be given to Zaid because it had the lowest average profit in this dataset.

## Conclusion

The analysis identified differences in yield, production, water efficiency, revenue, cost, and profit across crops, seasons, and irrigation methods. The results can help farmers and agricultural planners make better decisions about crop selection, irrigation, input use, and seasonal planning.
